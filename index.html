Honey Lost in space

Honey is lost in space, and sneaky Cat Enemies are trying to catch her! Help her dodge them, floating around. If the Cat Enemies catch Honey three times, its game over.

How to play

  • Press the left and right arrow keys to move Honey.
  • Press the up and down arrow keys to move her vertically.

Dodge the spaceships and help Honey escape!
