Honey goes to space

Honey is lost in space, and sneaky spaceships are trying to catch her! Help her dodge them, jump over floor obstacles, and collect bones to get some health back. If the spaceships catch Honey three times, its game over.

How to play

  • Press the left and right arrow keys to move Honey.
  • Press the up arrow key to jump over obstacles.
  • Collect bones to recover health.
  • Dodge the spaceships and help Honey escape!
